How to play Rival Guys

Stay on the platform while forcing opposing fighters out of the arena. A fighter that falls beyond the playable boundary is eliminated. Qualification rounds narrow the roster toward a final showdown.

Arena variety

Rival Guys includes six arena concepts with different spacing, hazards and movement challenges. The map roulette selects a different arena between rounds so matches are not limited to one layout.

Progression

Coins and gems earned through play can be used for character unlocks, upgrades and emotes. Progress is stored locally in your browser in the current self-hosted version.

FAQ

Rival Guys questions

Is Rival Guys free to play?

Yes. The browser version on masarpstudio.com can be played without purchasing the game.

Is Rival Guys online multiplayer?

The current version uses simulated opponents rather than real online matchmaking. Its player architecture is designed so networking can be added later without changing the core game loop.

Does Rival Guys work on mobile?

The web build includes touch controls and mobile UI layouts. Performance depends on the browser and device’s WebGL capability.

Who developed Rival Guys?

Rival Guys is developed by Samman Lamsal under MASARP STUDIO.
