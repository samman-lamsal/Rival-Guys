Privacy Policy

How MASARP STUDIO handles information on masarpstudio.com and its browser games.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

MASARP STUDIO provides games and information through masarpstudio.com. This policy explains the data used by the website, browser games and advertising services.

Local game data

Rival Guys currently stores gameplay progress such as coins, gems, unlocked fighters, upgrades, selected settings and emotes in your browser using local storage. This data is stored on your device and may be lost if you clear browser storage.

Advertising and cookies

If advertising is enabled, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers to serve, measure and improve ads. Google and its partners may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites, subject to your region, consent choices and Google settings.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. More information about how Google uses data on partner sites is available at Google’s partner-sites information page.

Consent

Where required by law, consent may be requested before certain cookies, local storage technologies or personalized advertising are used. Google advertising may require a Google-certified consent management platform for users in certain regions.

Third-party services

The site may link to external platforms such as Google Play. Those platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Children

The website is intended as a general-audience gaming site. We do not intentionally ask players to provide sensitive personal information through the Rival Guys browser game.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to sammanlamsal31@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site, advertising setup or game services change. The updated date above will be revised when material changes are made.
